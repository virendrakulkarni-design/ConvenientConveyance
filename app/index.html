Understanding Conveyancing Milestones

South Australia 5-Stage Electronic Conveyancing Workflow

Stage 1 Enquiry & Intake

Client engagement letter, Costs Disclosure Agreement, and initial identity collection. Checking whether the client is buying or selling, Torrens title type, and expected auction/private treaty timing.

Stage 2 Contract Signed

Contract exchanged by parties. Tracking the statutory 2 business day cooling-off period (or Section 66W waiver certificate). Collecting the 10% contract deposit into the stakeholder trust account.

Stage 3 Searches & Enquiries

Lodging mandatory statutory searches: SAILIS Certificate of Title, SA Water Section 7, Council rates, and Emergency Services Levy (ESL). Booking the SA Water Special Meter Reading (SMR) 14 business days before settlement.

Stage 4 Settlement Booked

Inviting incoming and discharging mortgagees into the PEXA digital workspace. Agreeing final Settlement Statement adjustments with other conveyancer and locking the net financial settlement schedule.

Stage 5 Completed

Electronic settlement executed through PEXA. Funds disbursed via RBA accounts, title transferred on SAILIS to the purchaser, keys handed over, and file archived.
